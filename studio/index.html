The Studio

Tools worth keeping.

A good tool disappears while you use it. You forget the software is even there and just get the work done. That’s the bar.

Fixin Studio is one person in Colorado making software for people who make things. We’d rather ship a few great tools than a hundred mediocre features. The rule is simple: if we wouldn’t reach for it every day, we don’t build it.

What we care about

  • It starts with a real problem, never a buzzword or a slide in a roadmap deck.
  • The details are the product: typography, loading states, the tiny copy nobody notices until it’s wrong.
  • Your data stays yours. We collect as little of it as humanly possible.
  • We’re building for ten years out, not next quarter’s metrics.

What we won’t build

No crypto, no gambling, no dark patterns, no ad-driven clickbait. We don’t sell customer data, and we don’t bolt on AI just to slap it on the box. If a feature doesn’t earn its keep, it doesn’t ship.

The promise

We won’t knowingly ship anything that wastes your time. When we get it wrong, we fix it. And every app we make has to be worth the space it takes on your phone. That’s the deal.
