The Studio

Tools worth keeping.

A good tool disappears while you use it. You stop thinking about the software and just do the work.

Fixin Studio is a small, independent software studio. We’d rather make a few things and make them well than ship a pile of features nobody asked for. If we wouldn’t use it ourselves, we don’t build it.

What we care about

  • Every product starts with an actual problem, not a buzzword.
  • The details are the product: typography, loading states, microcopy, accessibility.
  • We collect as little of your data as we can, and what’s yours stays yours.
  • We build for the long haul, not the next quarter.

What we won’t build

No crypto, no gambling, no dark patterns, no ad-driven clickbait. We don’t sell customer data, and we don’t bolt on AI just to have it on the box. If a feature doesn’t make the app better, it doesn’t ship.

The promise

We won’t knowingly ship something that wastes your time. When we get it wrong, we’ll fix it. Every app we make should be worth the space it takes on your phone.
