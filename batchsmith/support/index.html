Common questions

Are my recipes and batches safe?

BatchSmith stores your data on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details on what is and isn’t collected.

Is BatchSmith a substitute for food-safety guidance?

No. It’s a calculator and notebook, not professional advice. Please read the Food Safety Notice before you cure.

The cure amounts look off. What should I do?

Always weigh curing salt on a gram scale and verify against your cure manufacturer’s instructions and USDA/FSIS guidance. If you think a calculation is wrong, email us with the details.
