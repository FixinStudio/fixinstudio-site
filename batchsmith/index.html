BatchSmith.

Cure with confidence.

The no-nonsense companion for curing bacon and running your charcuterie like you know what you’re doing. Every batch, dialed in.

Curing shouldn’t be a guessing game. BatchSmith nails your salt and cure ratios, keeps every batch and recipe in one place, and remembers exactly what worked last time so you can do it again. Built for people who take the craft seriously. It stays out of the way while you do.

What it does

  • Exact cure amounts by weight. No volume guessing, no math errors.
  • Every batch tracked from mix to finish.
  • Notes, tweaks, and outcomes saved for each cure.
  • Target weight-loss tracking, so you know when it’s actually done.
  • Your whole charcuterie library in one place.
